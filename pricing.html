Questions about pricing

Can't find what you're looking for? Book a call and we'll talk it through.

Book a call
Do I have to buy the hardware?

Hardware is a one-off cost, separate from your monthly plan. A complete till kit, with a terminal, receipt printer, cash drawer and integrated card machine, costs £499 + VAT. We'll recommend exactly what your venue needs during your demo.

Can I add more tills, kiosks or screens?

Yes. Once you're on a plan, each extra licence is £19 a month + VAT, whether it's another till, a self-service kiosk, a kitchen display screen or a customer collection screen.

Which card machines does it work with?

OnePlusTwo works with Dojo, Viva.com, DNA Payments and Paynt. Your card rates are agreed directly with the provider you choose, and we'll help you get set up with the one that suits your business.

Is online ordering included?

Yes, on the Essentials and Premium plans. You get your own branded ordering website with collection, delivery, online payments and QR code table ordering, and orders go straight to your till and kitchen.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. As your business grows, you can move up to a bigger plan at any time. Just get in touch and we'll switch you over.

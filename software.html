OnePlusTwo's cloud-based software handles orders, payments, your kitchen and your reports. It's quick to learn and easy to change, so you stay in control of your menu and prices.

What you can do with it

Take orders quickly

A clear, customisable layout so staff find items in a tap, with options and notes for the kitchen.

Send orders to the kitchen

Orders go straight to kitchen printers or screens, so there's no running back and forth.

Take payments your way

Card, contactless and cash, plus split bills and tips, all recorded in one place.

Manage tables and orders

See which tables are free, what's been ordered and what's waiting to be paid.

Track stock

Know what's running low before it runs out, and see what sells best.

See live reports

Sales, busy times and staff performance on your phone or laptop, wherever you are.

Change menus in minutes

Update prices, add specials or remove sold-out items without calling anyone.

Keep trading offline

If the internet drops, the tills carry on and sync when it's back.

Manage more than one site

Run several venues or stalls from one account.

Check on your venue from anywhere

Because everything runs in the cloud, you can see today's takings, update your menu or check stock from home, the office or another venue.

Why add a driver collection screen

A clearer counter

Drivers check the screen instead of queueing at the counter, so walk-in customers can still get served.

Fewer interruptions

Your team spends less time answering "is it ready yet?" and more time getting orders out.

Quicker handovers

Drivers can see the moment an order is ready, so food goes out hot.

How it works

Delivery orders show as they're being prepared. When the kitchen marks an order done, it moves to ready, so drivers know exactly when to come up.

What is a driver collection screen?

A screen for delivery drivers and couriers that shows which orders are being prepared and which are ready to collect, so they don't need to queue at the counter or ask staff.

Do I need a special screen?

No. It runs on a TV or monitor of your choice, and we'll advise on what works best for your space.

How much does it cost?

£19 a month + VAT per screen when you're on a OnePlusTwo plan.

Can I have a customer collection screen as well?

Yes. Many takeaways use one screen for customers and another for drivers. Each is £19 a month + VAT.

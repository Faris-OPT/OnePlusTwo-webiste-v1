How it works

The Artech VR1 caller ID box connects to your restaurant's VoIP phone line. When a call comes in, the caller's number fills in on your till. For repeat customers, their name and address show up too.

What is caller ID on a POS system?

Caller ID fills in the caller's phone number on your till as the phone rings. If they've ordered before, their name and address appear too, so staff can take phone orders quickly without asking for details again.

What do I need for caller ID?

A VoIP phone line, the Artech VR1 caller ID box (£69 + VAT, one-off) and the Essentials plan. The VR1 is needed for caller ID to work.

Which plans include caller ID?

Caller ID is included in our Essentials plan at £79 a month + VAT. Premium includes everything in Essentials, so it's there too.

Does caller ID work with a normal landline?

No. Caller ID works with VoIP phone lines. If you're not sure what kind of line you have, ask us during your demo.

Let customers know the moment their order is ready

A customer collection screen shows every order number as Preparing or Ready, so people can relax instead of crowding the counter, and your team can get on with the next order.

Book a demoSee pricing

Why add a collection screen

A calmer counter

Customers wait comfortably and watch for their number, instead of queueing to ask about their order.

Fewer interruptions

Staff spend less time answering 'is mine ready?' and more time serving.

Perfect with kiosks

Customers order at a kiosk, take their number and watch it move from Preparing to Ready.

How it works

When an order is placed, its number appears under Preparing. When the kitchen marks it done, it moves to Ready, so customers know exactly when to come up.

  • Choose which orders appear, usually kiosk and till orders
  • Runs on a TV or monitor of your choice
  • Updates automatically from your kitchen

Book a meeting at EuroCIS

Show appointments are booked through the same calendar as our online demos. Here's how to book a meeting with us at the show.

  1. Open our booking calendar using the button below.
  2. Pick a date from 16 to 18 February 2027 and a time that suits you.
  3. Let us know it's a show appointment. After booking, email hello@oneplustwo.co.uk with the subject "EuroCIS show appointment", so we know you're meeting us at Hall 5, Booth 5A10 rather than booking an online demo.

EPOS and POS glossary

Plain-English explanations of the words you'll come across when choosing a till, a kiosk or a card machine.

BCDEHIKLMOPQSTV

B

Barcode scanner
A device that reads product barcodes at the till, for fast, accurate checkouts with fewer errors. Popular with retailers. Learn more about Barcode scanner

C

Caller ID
A feature that fills in the caller's phone number on your till as the phone rings. With OnePlusTwo, repeat customers' names and addresses appear too. It works with VoIP phone lines and the Artech VR1 caller ID box. Learn more about Caller ID
Chip & PIN
A way of paying by card where the customer inserts their card and enters a PIN on the card machine. Learn more about Chip & PIN
Cloud POS
A point of sale system that stores your sales, menus and reports online rather than on one machine, so you can change menus and check sales from anywhere. Learn more about Cloud POS
Collection display screen
A screen that shows customers when their order is being prepared and when it's ready to collect, so they wait calmly instead of crowding the counter. Learn more about Collection display screen
Contactless payment
Paying by tapping a card, phone or watch on a card reader, without entering a PIN for most amounts. Learn more about Contactless payment
Customer display screen
A second screen facing the customer, so they can check their order as it's rung through. Learn more about Customer display screen

D

Delivery app integration
A connection that sends orders from delivery apps such as Uber Eats, Deliveroo and Just Eat straight into your till and kitchen, so nothing needs re-typing. Learn more about Delivery app integration
Digital signage
Screens that show your menu, offers and specials, and are easy to update. Also called menu screens. Learn more about Digital signage

E

EPOS
Short for electronic point of sale. Software and hardware that replace an old-style cash register, taking payments, tracking sales and stock, and keeping your data in one place in real time. Learn more about EPOS

H

Handheld POS
A portable till that staff carry around the venue, so they can take orders and payments at the table or in a queue. Learn more about Handheld POS

I

Integrated card machine
A card machine linked to your till, so the total is sent automatically and there's nothing to type in. OnePlusTwo works with Dojo, Viva.com, DNA Payments and Paynt. Learn more about Integrated card machine

K

Kitchen display screen (KDS)
A screen in the kitchen that shows orders in real time instead of printed tickets, sent to the right station, with changes and cancellations showing up instantly. Learn more about Kitchen display screen (KDS)

L

Loyalty scheme
A way to reward regular customers through your till, without paper stamp cards. Included in OnePlusTwo Essentials and Premium. Learn more about Loyalty scheme

M

Menu screens
See digital signage. Learn more about Menu screens

O

Online ordering website
Your own branded website where customers order for collection or delivery and pay online, with orders going straight to your till and kitchen. Learn more about Online ordering website

P

Point of sale (POS)
The place, and the system, where a customer pays. In hospitality, a POS system also takes orders and sends them to the kitchen. Learn more about Point of sale (POS)

Q

QR code table ordering
Customers scan a QR code at their table to see the menu, order and pay on their own phone. Learn more about QR code table ordering

S

Self-service kiosk
A touchscreen where customers browse the menu, order and pay on their own, cutting queues and freeing up staff. Learn more about Self-service kiosk
Split bill
Dividing one bill between guests, by item or by guest, at the till. Learn more about Split bill
Stock control
Tracking stock levels as items sell, with alerts when it's time to reorder. Learn more about Stock control

T

Table bookings
Online reservations where guests choose a date, time, table area and table, with table status shown live from your restaurant. Learn more about Table bookings
Tap to Pay via NFC
Taking contactless payments using the till or tablet itself as the card reader, with no separate card machine. The Sunmi V3 Mix works this way. Learn more about Tap to Pay via NFC
Thermal printer
A fast printer that uses heat-sensitive paper for customer receipts and kitchen tickets. Most POS systems use 80mm paper rolls. Learn more about Thermal printer
Till kit
A complete set of POS hardware: the till, a receipt printer, a cash drawer and an integrated card machine. Learn more about Till kit

V

VoIP
Short for Voice over Internet Protocol. A phone line that runs over the internet instead of a traditional landline. OnePlusTwo caller ID works with VoIP lines. Learn more about VoIP

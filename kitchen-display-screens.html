What is a kitchen display system (KDS)?

A KDS shows orders on screens in your kitchen in real time, instead of printed tickets. It keeps orders clear, speeds up service and improves communication between front of house and the kitchen.

Can I use more than one screen?

Yes. Use as many as you need for different stations, such as starters, mains, desserts or the bar, and orders are sent to the right one automatically.

Can I use screens and printers together?

Yes. Many kitchens use screens for most stations and a printer for others, or keep a printer as a backup.

Can I customise the screens?

Yes. You can adjust the layout, colour-code orders and set up screens for each station to match your kitchen's workflow.

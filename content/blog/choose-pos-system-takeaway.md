A takeaway's busiest hour is chaos for a till that wasn't built for it. Walk-ins, phone orders, delivery apps and your own website all arrive at once, and every one needs to reach the kitchen quickly and correctly. Here's what to check before you choose.

## 1. Delivery apps straight into the till

If you take orders from Uber Eats, Deliveroo or Just Eat, you don't want a separate tablet for each app and someone re-typing orders. Look for a system where delivery app orders go straight into your till and kitchen, alongside everything else. We also work with Uber Direct and Otter. See all our [integrations](/integrations).

## 2. Your own online ordering website

Delivery apps bring new customers, but orders through your own website are yours. Check whether the system includes a branded ordering site with collection, delivery and online payments, and whether orders land in the same queue as the rest. With us, an [online ordering website](/online-ordering) is included in the Essentials and Premium plans.

## 3. Phone orders that don't get lost

Plenty of regulars still ring through, and phone orders scribbled on a pad are easy to lose. Check how the system handles phone orders. Our Essentials plan includes a phone (VoIP) integration, and we'll show you how it works with your phone line during your demo.

## 4. Kitchen display screens

Paper tickets get lost, splashed and misread. A [kitchen display screen](/kitchen-display-screens) shows every order the moment it's placed, sent to the right station. Ours start from £399 + VAT for the screen, plus £19 a month + VAT per screen.

## 5. A collection screen for a calmer counter

If customers wait in the shop, a [collection display screen](/collection-display-screens) shows each order number as Preparing or Ready, so people stop queueing to ask "is mine ready?". It runs on a TV or monitor of your choice for £19 a month + VAT.

## 6. Reports across every channel

At the end of the night you want one set of numbers covering walk-in, phone, website and delivery app sales, rather than five separate logins. Check you can see live sales on your phone, too.

## 7. Hardware that suits your counter

Most takeaways need a sturdy countertop till, a receipt printer, a cash drawer and an integrated card machine. Our complete till kits start from £379 + VAT. The [hardware shop](/shop) lists every option.

## 8. Support when you're open

Takeaways trade late and at weekends, so check when support is actually available. Ours is 7 days a week.

## Questions to ask any supplier

- Which delivery apps connect directly to the till, and is there an extra charge?
- Is an online ordering website included, or is it extra?
- What does the hardware cost, and is it a one-off or monthly?
- What happens to orders if the internet drops?
- When is support available?

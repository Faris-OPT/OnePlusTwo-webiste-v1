What are integrated payments?

It means your till and card machine talk to each other. The amount is sent straight to the payment terminal and every transaction is recorded automatically, with no manual entry.

Which payment providers do you work with?

OnePlusTwo works with Dojo, Viva.com, DNA Payments and Paynt.

How much are the card fees?

Card rates are set by the payment provider you choose, and vary from provider to provider. We'll help you get set up with the one that suits your business.

What payment methods can I accept?

Cash and all popular debit and credit cards, including contactless and mobile wallets.

Can I take payments away from the counter?

Yes. With tap to pay on a phone or tablet, or a portable card machine, you can take payments at the table, in the queue or anywhere on site.

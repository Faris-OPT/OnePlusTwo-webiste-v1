What is an EPOS system?

EPOS stands for electronic point of sale. It replaces an old-style cash register with software and hardware that take payments, track sales and stock, and keep your customer data in one place, in real time.

Does it work across more than one location?

Yes. You can manage several sites from one account, and the Premium plan includes multi-store features and an assigned account manager.

Can it take orders from delivery apps?

Yes. OnePlusTwo works with Uber Eats, Deliveroo and Just Eat, so delivery orders come straight into your till and kitchen.

What support is available?

Our team provides support 7 days a week.

How can a self-service kiosk help my business?

Kiosks raise the average spend per order by suggesting extras, cut waiting times at busy periods and let your staff focus on making and serving. Many customers also simply prefer ordering this way.

Can I customise the colour of my kiosk?

Yes. Kiosks can be tailored to suit your venue and your brand. Get in touch and we'll talk through the options.

Is it difficult to install?

Not at all. Kiosks come with a clear installation guide and are simple to put together, and we're on hand if you need help.

Is it easy for customers to use?

Yes. The layout is simple and intuitive for customers of all ages, and there's an accessibility mode for customers with disabilities.

What support is available?

Our team provides support 7 days a week.
